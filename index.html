How to play

Move with ← → or A / D (or the on-screen buttons). Fire with Space or the FIRE button. Only one of your bullets flies at a time — make it count. Destroy the whole fleet to clear the level; each level gets faster. Hide behind the green shields — they erode when hit. Shoot the red UFO for a bonus (50–300 pts). You earn an extra life at 1,000 points.
